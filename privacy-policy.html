Your Privacy is Our Priority

GETINSURE CRM stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with any server or third party. This app works 100% offline.

1. Who We Are

GETINSURE CRM ("the App", "we", "us") is a sales CRM application designed exclusively for independent insurance agents to manage leads, follow-ups, and the sales pipeline. The App is developed and published by GETINSURE.

Contact: smiletradensk@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

The App collects only the information that you manually enter:

  • Lead details — name, mobile number, city, product interest, status, source, and notes
  • Interaction records — call logs, meeting notes, follow-up schedules
  • Your account details — full name, email address, and a 4-digit PIN (stored locally)
  • App preferences — font size setting

We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, analytics, crash reports, usage statistics, or any other data beyond what you explicitly enter into the App.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored exclusively on your device using:

  • SQLite database (via expo-sqlite) — for leads and interaction records
  • AsyncStorage — for account credentials (name, email, PIN) and preferences

Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the "Export CSV", "Export JSON", or "Share Backup" features — in which case you choose where to send it (WhatsApp, Google Drive, email, etc.). GETINSURE does not receive a copy.

ℹ️ There is no GETINSURE server. The app has no backend, no API calls, and no cloud sync.

4. Contacts Permission

The App requests access to your device contacts solely for the "Import from Contacts" feature. When you use this feature:

  • You choose which contacts to import by selecting them in the app
  • Only the selected contacts' names and phone numbers are saved locally as leads
  • Contact data is never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party
  • You can revoke this permission at any time: Settings → Apps → GETINSURE CRM → Permissions → Contacts → Deny

We do not scan, read, or store your full contacts list. Access is used only during the import action you initiate.

5. Notifications Permission

The App requests notification permission to send you follow-up reminders for leads you have scheduled. Notifications are generated entirely on-device and do not involve any server. You can disable notifications at any time in your phone's Settings.

6. Camera & Storage Permissions

Camera and storage permissions are requested only if you use image-sharing features. Photos are accessed at the time of sharing and are not uploaded or stored by the App beyond your own device's local storage.

7. Auto-Backup Feature

The optional Auto-Backup feature saves a JSON file of your leads to a folder you choose on your device (typically Downloads). This file:

  • Stays on your device only — it is not uploaded anywhere automatically
  • Can be manually shared by you to Google Drive, WhatsApp, or email
  • Can be deleted at any time by removing the backup folder or uninstalling the App

8. No Third-Party Services

GETINSURE CRM does not integrate with any analytics platforms, advertising networks, crash-reporting services, or cloud databases. There are no third-party SDKs that collect your personal data.

The Facebook Live Leads feature is an optional webhook configuration that you set up yourself. If you configure it, your webhook server (which you control) receives leads from Facebook. GETINSURE does not operate or have access to this server.

9. Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • 4-digit PIN authentication to prevent unauthorised access to the App
  • Android device-level encryption (if enabled on your phone)
  • No network transmission — no risk of interception in transit

We recommend always locking your device screen. If your device is lost or stolen and is not PIN/biometric protected at the OS level, someone with physical access could potentially access the App.

10. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the App (removes the SQLite database and all stored data)
  • Clearing App storage: Settings → Apps → GETINSURE CRM → Storage → Clear Data
  • Logging out and creating a new account (data remains per email account on the device)

Since all data is only on your device, deletion is immediate and irreversible. We hold no copy of your data anywhere.

11. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for use by professional insurance agents and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used this App, please uninstall it and clear the App's storage data from Android Settings.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
