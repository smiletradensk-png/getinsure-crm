FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
------------------------------------
GETINSURE CRM is the ultimate sales management tool built exclusively for independent insurance agents and advisors. Manage your entire sales pipeline — from first contact to policy closure — right from your phone, with zero subscription fees and no internet required.

🔒 100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE
Your data never leaves your device. No cloud sync, no server, no subscription. All lead data is stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database. Your clients' information stays yours.

👥 LEAD MANAGEMENT
• Add unlimited leads with name, mobile, product, status, source, city, and notes
• Track 6 lead statuses: New → Interested → Follow-up → Pipeline → Closed / Not Interested
• Search and filter leads instantly by name, mobile, or city
• Status filter tabs: view all New leads, Interested clients, or Closed deals at a glance
• Bulk WhatsApp messaging with personalised templates — send to multiple leads at once
• One-tap Call or WhatsApp directly from the lead card

📅 FOLLOW-UP MANAGEMENT
• Schedule follow-ups with date, time, and type (Call / WhatsApp / Meeting / Visit / Email)
• Three smart views: Overdue, Today, and Upcoming follow-ups
• Get local push notifications so you never miss a follow-up
• Mark follow-ups complete and move leads to the next stage

📊 PIPELINE & DASHBOARD
• Kanban-style pipeline view across all sales stages
• Dashboard with live stats: total leads, closing rate, today's tasks
• Interested, Overdue, Follow-up, and Closed count cards at a glance
• Pipeline completion percentages per stage

📱 IMPORT LEADS FROM MULTIPLE SOURCES
• Import from Phone Contacts — select contacts and import as leads instantly
• Import from CSV — supports Facebook Ads, IndiaMart, WhatsApp Business, Google Sheets exports
• Facebook Live Leads webhook — receive Facebook Lead Ads automatically in real-time
• Bulk CSV import with intelligent column detection

💾 BACKUP & EXPORT
• Export leads to CSV or JSON — share via WhatsApp, Email, or Google Drive
• Auto-Backup to Downloads folder — daily automatic backup with one-time setup
• Restore from backup — import leads back after reinstalling
• Share Backup — manually share a full backup to Google Drive or WhatsApp

🎨 PREMIUM FINTECH DESIGN
• Deep navy and gold color scheme inspired by professional banking apps
• Clean card-based UI with smooth gradients
• Adjustable font sizes: Small, Normal, Large, XL
• Dark gradient headers with hero stats strip

🔐 ACCOUNT & SECURITY
• Email + 4-digit PIN login — no OTP, no phone number required
• Multiple accounts on one device — each with separate data
• Forgot PIN recovery via registered email
• All credentials stored locally only

WHO IS THIS FOR?
• LIC agents and advisors
• Private insurance agents (Health, Motor, Life, Term)
• ULIP and Mutual Fund advisors
• Any freelance financial advisor managing a client pipeline

WHAT PRODUCTS CAN YOU TRACK?
Whole Life, Child Plans, Retirement Plans, Health Insurance, Motor Insurance, Travel Insurance, Home Insurance, Corporate Insurance, ULIP, Endowment, SIP, Mutual Fund, and more.

No subscription. No ads. No cloud. Just a powerful local CRM that works for you.

Download GETINSURE CRM today and take control of your insurance sales pipeline.
